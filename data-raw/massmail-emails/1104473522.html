2026 Campus Charitable Fund Drive
September 21, 2026 9:06 AM

Dear Colleagues,

One of the defining characteristics of Illinois is that it is a community led by students, staff, and faculty deeply committed to making a meaningful impact on our state and beyond.

The Campus Charitable Fund Drive, our annual workplace giving campaign, begins today and will run through Nov. 13. In the coming weeks, university employees will have the opportunity to support the charities of their choice through several giving options. A gift of any size truly makes a difference.

Our annual contributions to the CCFD are a meaningful way for Illinois to support the communities around us. This year, we have set a goal of raising $1 million to support public-serving nonprofit organizations in our community and beyond. With your participation, I know we can achieve it.

I also invite you to attend the 2026 CCFD Agency Fair today, Sept. 21, from 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m., in the Illini Union, rooms 103 and 104. The fair is a great opportunity to meet representatives from the organizations supported by CCFD and to learn more about how your contributions make a difference.

Thank you for helping us shape a better future, together.

Sincerely,

Charles L. Isbell, Jr.
Chancellor
