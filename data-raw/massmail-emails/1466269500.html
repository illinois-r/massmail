Take action before Dec. 18 to prevent losing Wi-Fi access
November 3, 2026 11:02 AM

Individuals using personally owned devices (computers, tablets, phones, etc.) and some university-owned or managed devices need to update their Wi-Fi certificate before the end of the semester to prevent losing access to Illinois Wi-Fi services including IllinoisNet and eduroam. If you are using a university-owned or managed device, your IT professional will contact you to assist with this change. 
 
What you need to do 

Renew your certificate on your personal devices for IllinoisNet and/or eduroam by running the JoinNow tool, which will guide you through steps to renew your certificate and reconnect to Illinois Wi-Fi: 

  • Renew your certificate for only IllinoisNet: go.illinois.edu/illinoisnetsetup.

  • Renew your certificate for IllinoisNet and eduroam: go.illinois.edu/eduroamsetup. 

You are encouraged to renew your certificate before Dec. 18 to prevent Wi-Fi disruptions for the beginning of the spring semester. Renewing your certificate and validating connectivity requires you to be on campus using IllinoisNet.  

Who this affects

  • All personally owned devices (computers, tablets, phones, etc.).
  • Some university-owned or managed devices. University-owned or managed devices may or may not need to be updated depending on if the Wi-Fi is set up to be managed by your department. Your IT professional will contact you if you need to make this change. 
  • This does not affect IllinoisNet_Guest.  

What happens if you don't renew your certificate before the end of the semester

Devices that are not updated by the end of the calendar year will need to renew certificates when returning to campus. If you haven’t renewed your certificate by the end of the semester, you may begin to see pop-up errors saying IllinoisNet or eduroam is untrusted or unavailable, and you will not be able to connect to Wi-Fi. You will need to renew your certificate on the device before you can connect to the network. 

Questions

If you have questions, submit a request to the IT Service Desk or visit one of our locations in-person.  

To see whether you have renewed the certificate correctly, visit this Answers KnowledgeBase article.
